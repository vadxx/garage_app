# Changelog

## 1.0.0-rc.1 - 2026-07-08

Initial release of garage_app.

### Features
- Garage management — add, edit and remove cars with mileage, value and color.
- Maintenance log — record oil changes, repairs, fuel, tires, cleaning, insurance and more.
- Spending overview — total spent per car and top spending category.
- Oil health — monitor engine oil life based on mileage and a configurable interval.
- CSV import/export — back up, restore or seed data from a CSV file.
- Offline-first — all data stored locally; no account or internet required.
- Multi-language & currency support with km/mi distance units.
