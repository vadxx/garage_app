# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-07-08

This Privacy Policy applies to the **garage_app** mobile application.

## Data Collection

garage_app does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

All data you enter into the app (cars, maintenance records, settings, etc.) is stored **locally on your device** only. The app does not connect to the internet, does not use analytics, and does not display ads.

## Permissions

The app may request access to your device's file storage solely for importing or exporting your own data via CSV files. This access is used only at your request and no files are uploaded anywhere.

## Third-Party Services

garage_app does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect data.

## Children's Privacy

garage_app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
